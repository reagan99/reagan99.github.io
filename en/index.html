MAKE ROOM FOR YOUR TOOLS

Add the tools you need
with extensions.

Find and install extensions from Open VSX, then open them in your workspace. SSH workspaces use the runtime on your chosen server; local workspaces use the capabilities supported on your device.

Available features depend on the APIs and runtime each extension requires. Check the publisher and access scope before activating an extension.
